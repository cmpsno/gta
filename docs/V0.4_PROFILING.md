# V0.4 Profiling — Measured Scale

Measured 2026-09-20 on this dev VM (headless sim via `sandbox_bench`, render
via `palm_district --smoke --npcs N` under Xvfb).

## Simulation cost vs NPC count

`sandbox_bench` runs 1200 steps (10 simulated seconds) of a sprinting player
plus N route-walking NPCs, 120 Hz fixed timestep, O2.

| npcs | total ms | µs / step | box checks / step |
|------|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1    | 3.52     | 2.93      | 48.0              |
| 10   | 18.08    | 15.07     | 245.9             |
| 25   | 42.24    | 35.20     | 583.3             |
| 50   | 80.68    | 67.24     | 1139.4            |
| 100  | 159.09   | 132.57    | 2238.9            |

Cost per step is linear in NPC count: ~1.3 µs per NPC per step. Each NPC
adds ~22 box-overlap checks per step (substepped axis-separated movement
against the 12 building boxes).

At the 120 Hz timestep, 100 NPCs consume ~16 ms of CPU per real second —
1.6% of the frame budget. The current walker design is two orders of
magnitude below anything that would threaten the timestep.

## Render cost vs NPC count

`palm_district --smoke --npcs N` (120 frames, 60 Hz sim pacing) under Xvfb
(software rasterizer — absolute numbers are not representative of a GPU):

| scene  | npcs | avg frame ms |
|--------|------|--------------|
| street | 1    | 61.6         |
| street | 100  | 51.4         |
| drive  | 1    | 41.1         |

Run-to-run variance under llvmpipe is ±10 ms, so the 1→100 NPC delta is
noise, not signal: 99 extra box-primitive NPCs add no measurable render
cost. Each NPC is ~10 draw calls of untextured boxes, same batch cost class
as the existing buildings. The render side is not the scaling constraint;
the sim side (above) is, and it has two orders of magnitude of headroom.

## Interpretation

- Box checks are O(buildings) per move substep, and buildings is 12. There
  is no spatial-index pressure at any shipped or plausible NPC count.
- Linear per-NPC cost (~1.3 µs) means even 500 NPCs would cost ~0.65 ms per
  step — still under 8% of the 8.33 ms budget at 120 Hz. Traffic AI (dozens
  of vehicles + pedestrians with waypoints) fits comfortably in the existing
  architecture.
- **Recommendation: GO.** No quadtree, no broadphase, no data-layout work.
  The next perf task, if any, is render-side (draw calls per NPC), measured
  above.
